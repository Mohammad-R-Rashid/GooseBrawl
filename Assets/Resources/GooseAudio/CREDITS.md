# Audio credits

All recordings are public domain / CC0.

- `honks/honk_01.wav` … `honk_16.wav`: single Canada goose honks sliced from U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service and
  National Park Service field recordings (public domain).
- `yellowstone_canada_geese.mp3`: Canada geese flock, Yellowstone National Park (NPS / MSU Acoustic Atlas, public domain).
- `geese_honking_distant.ogg`: distant geese (Wikimedia Commons, CC0).

Everything else you hear (webbed footsteps, wing flaps, air whooshes, the flap-dash push-off, the tackle, the egg crack
and shell scatter, the shell tap, twigs settling, button taps, the heartbeat and the breathing) is synthesized at
startup by `Assets/Scripts/Audio/ProceduralAudio.cs` at 44.1 kHz. There is no music in the game, by design.

# GOOSED. — Hackathon Build

An iPhone AR chase game built with Unity 6 (6000.6), URP, AR Foundation 6.6 and the ARKit XR Plug-in.
You scan your floor, place a goose nest, steal the egg, and a ridiculous goose chases you through
your real room. The goose lives in AR world space: it is behind you, you hear it and feel it, and if
you turn the phone around it is right there, casting a shadow on your floor.

Everything is one scene, one game mode, no server, no accounts. The only persistent data is the best
score / best time, games played and the sound / haptics toggles in `PlayerPrefs`.

(The code, the scene and the bundle id still say Goose Brawl / eggsnatcher on purpose: renaming them would wipe
saved best times on installed phones. Only the user-visible name changed.)

## Design rules (do not change without asking)

- **No background music.** Ever. Only diegetic sound: the goose, your heartbeat and breathing, the crack, the flock, foley.
- Survival time is the score; no points.
- Portrait, one-handed. The goose is a real AR object, never a UI overlay.
- Graphics must read as mature and immersive: real shadows, post-processing, room-matched light, real reflections.
  No decals, no red screen paint, no primitives-in-a-trench-coat props.
- The goose's real speed stays gentle (1.1 -> 1.6 m/s). Urgency comes from flap-dashes, animation speed, honk cadence and feathers.

## Game flow (state machine)

`Boot -> ScanEnvironment -> PlaceNest -> EggReady -> EggStolen -> Chasing <-> Danger -> JumpAttack -> GameOver -> (Run again) EggReady`

The EggStolen beat: you grab the egg ("BREAKFAST TIME!"), it flies into your hand ("GOT IT. LET'S GO COOK."), then it slips,
drops in slow motion (the picture desaturates) and cracks on the floor in front of you: jagged shell pieces, yolk and a white
that spreads out. The crack is what the goose hears. It flies in low **along your current line of sight** with a continuous
wing-beat, lands 2.4-3.2 m in front of you and **glares**: it does not move until it has been in view for half a second (or
4.5 s pass). Then the HUD says **TURN AROUND AND RUN!** and there is a 2 s grace period. On repeat rounds the steal beat plays
faster and a tap skips ahead. The classic spawn-behind-you entrance is still available by turning off `GooseGameManager.flyInEntrance`.

## One-time setup

The Editor menu **Goose Brawl > Setup Project** does everything. It also runs automatically when
`GooseBrawlAutoSetup.SetupVersion` changes (see `GooseBrawlSetup.cs`). It:

1. Adds the `AREnvironment` and `Interactable` layers.
2. Configures iOS player settings (product name GOOSED, home-screen label GOOSED. via the post-build step, camera usage description, portrait, IL2CPP/ARM64, iOS 16+, bundle id).
3. Enables the ARKit loader in XR Plug-in Management and marks ARKit as required.
4. Adds `ARBackgroundRendererFeature` to every URP renderer (needed for the camera feed).
5. Configures the render pipeline assets: render scale 1, MSAA 4x, soft shadows at 2048, depth texture, transparent shadow
   receive, and creates `Assets/Settings/GoosedPostFX.asset`.
6. Sets audio import settings (honks as PCM, the flock recording streamed).
7. Imports TextMeshPro essential resources (UI falls back to legacy Text if that fails).
8. Creates the materials (re-applying every property, so tuning in `GooseBrawlMaterials.cs` always takes effect), bakes the goose
   occlusion map, generates the app icon and launch screen, imports the goose FBX as a Generic rig with looping clips, builds `Goose.prefab`.
9. Builds `Assets/Scenes/GooseBrawl.unity` (AR Session, XR Origin + AR camera with post-processing, plane/raycast/mesh/occlusion/
   environment-probe managers, post-FX Volume, game services, UI, key light).
10. Adds the scene to Build Settings, runs **Goose Brawl > Validate Project**, and switches the build target to iOS.

**Goose Brawl > Validate Project** prints a PASS/WARN/FAIL report in the Console.

### Why the post-FX profile has every effect switched on

URP strips Uber shader variants from the build for every post-processing effect that is not *active* in some
`VolumeProfile` **asset**. `CinematicLookController` drives lens distortion, chromatic aberration, vignette and colour at
runtime on the auto-cloned profile instance, so the asset keeps small non-zero baselines to keep the variants alive.
Do not zero them and do not add Tonemapping (the camera feed is already display-referred; it would grade the real world).

## Testing in the Editor (mock AR)

Press Play in `GooseBrawl.unity`. In the Editor `GooseGameManager.useMockARInEditor` (USE_MOCK_AR) is on,
so a mock room (floor, two walls, a couch, a table, a pillar on the `AREnvironment` layer) and a
first-person mock camera replace AR Foundation.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D / arrows | move |
| Q / E | turn |
| right mouse drag | look |
| left click | tap (place nest, tap egg, skip the steal beat on repeat rounds) |
| Space | Start / Steal egg / Resume |
| R | Run again (on game over) |

The mock exercises the whole loop: scanning, placement, egg theft, fly-in, glare, chase, flap-dashes, obstacle
avoidance, honks, haptics (counted), scoring, lunge and game over. `GooseBrawlRemote` (Editor only) polls
`Library/GooseBrawlCommand.txt` for `play`, `smoke`, `stop`, `setup`, `validate`, `save`, `build`, `settings`; the
smoke test also saves screenshots of every beat to `Library/SmokeShots/`.

## Building for iPhone

1. **Goose Brawl > Build iOS Xcode Project (Development)** builds `Builds/iOS/Unity-iPhone.xcodeproj`
   (or use File > Build Profiles > iOS; Setup already switched the target and added the scene).
2. Player Settings that matter are pre-set: camera usage description, portrait, Metal, IL2CPP, ARM64,
   minimum iOS 16.0, bundle id `com.goosebrawl.eggsnatcher` (change it to your team's if needed).
3. Open the Xcode project, set your Team under Signing & Capabilities, plug in the iPhone and Run. From the
   command line (automatic signing, replace the team id and device UDID):

```bash
xcodebuild -project Builds/iOS/Unity-iPhone.xcodeproj -scheme Unity-iPhone -configuration Debug \
  -destination "platform=iOS,id=<UDID>" -derivedDataPath Builds/DerivedData \
  -allowProvisioningUpdates -allowProvisioningDeviceRegistration DEVELOPMENT_TEAM=<TEAMID> CODE_SIGN_STYLE=Automatic build
xcrun devicectl device install app --device <CoreDevice-id> "Builds/DerivedData/Build/Products/Debug-iphoneos/GOOSED..app"
xcrun devicectl device process launch --device <CoreDevice-id> --console com.goosebrawl.eggsnatcher
```

4. On first launch accept the camera permission.

See KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md for what was and was not verified on the device.

## Look, sound, feel

- **Rendering**: MSAA 4x at full render scale (alpha-to-coverage on the goose's feather cards), URP post-processing
  (bloom on highlights, dark vignette, gentle grade, film grain; no tonemapping), real soft shadows from the goose, egg and
  nest onto an invisible **shadow catcher** on your floor (`GooseBrawl/ShadowCatcher`: ZTest off, virtual occluders via the
  depth prepass, real occluders via the ARKit environment depth with a soft tolerance so LiDAR noise never speckles), a
  multiplicative contact patch under the goose, ARKit **light estimation** driving the key light and ambient (lumens +
  colour temperature; `ARLightRig`), ARKit **environment probes** for real reflections on the egg and the goose's beak and eyes.
- **Goose material**: 2048 albedo, full-res normal, metallic/smoothness, baked occlusion (from the packed ORM map), double
  sided on the shared asset; a grey variant shows up on roughly one round in three.
- **Props**: lathe egg with a speckled shell; nest = lumpy lathe bowl + one combined mesh of ~80 woven strands with a generated
  normal map + down feathers + a warm point light while the egg is home; the cracked egg = two jagged shell halves, six shards,
  a yolk dome and a spreading white. Feathers flutter with a noise field; dust puffs on footsteps and landings.
- **State grade** (`CinematicLookController`): danger darkens and reddens the vignette; impacts punch the lens and add
  chromatic aberration; slow motion desaturates; rage desaturates slightly. No red UI paint, only a subtle HUD jitter.
- **UI**: title and results in the cream / yolk / brown brand; everything over the live camera is dark glass with light
  condensed type. The **locator** pins a goose icon, an arrow and the distance to the screen edge whenever the goose is
  off-screen (BEHIND YOU at the bottom when it is behind the phone) and pulses on every honk. Comic HONK bubbles pop only
  while the goose is on screen. First run shows a three-step coaching card with a space-safety line. Pause (button or when
  the app goes to the background), sound and haptics toggles, MOVE NEST from the results screen.
- **Audio**: the honks are real geese (sixteen slices, shuffle-bag so nothing repeats). Everything else is synthesized at
  44.1 kHz in round-robin variants: webbed footsteps, wing flaps and a wing-beat loop for the fly-in, whooshes, the flap-dash
  push-off, the tackle (thud + feathers + beak clacks), the egg crack with shell scatter, shell tap, twigs settling, button
  taps, a heartbeat that is audible on a phone speaker, breathing. Goose sources use a custom rolloff and a **low-pass that
  muffles the goose when it is behind you**; a mild room reverb zone sits at the nest; slow motion drops pitch and cutoff.
  The distant flock joins in rage mode. There is deliberately no music.
- **Haptics** (`HapticsService` + `GooseHaptics.mm`, Core Haptics): the heartbeat pattern fires on the same tick as the
  heartbeat sound with intensity following danger; footsteps are felt within 3 m; honks buzz when close; wing beats during the
  fly-in; lunge wind-up ramp, launch, landing; the tackle; the egg crack; rage rumble; success on a new best. Two rate-limit
  channels keep ambient ticks from swallowing events.

## Scoring

Survival **time** is the score. The HUD shows the timer big, plus honks survived and your best time. The game-over card shows
the time big, honks survived and the best time. Best time and games played are the only persistent data (PlayerPrefs, via `ScoreManager`).

## Tuning

All the interesting numbers are serialized fields:

- `GooseChaseController`: walk/run/max speed (gentle by design), time to max speed, walk phase, catch distance, lunge trigger
  distance, replan rate, honk intervals, angry flap interval, think pauses; **Entrance**: fly-in distance/height/speed, glare seen
  duration, glare max duration, grace period; **Escalation**: tier start times (8/18/30 s), dash cooldown per tier, dash
  min/max/stop distance, rage speed/honk multipliers.
- `GooseAttackController`: lunge height/duration/cooldown, catch radius, minimum chase time before a lunge (dash values live in `HopSpec.Dash`).
- `GooseObstacleAvoidance`: body radius/height, look-ahead, emergency stop distance, scoring weights.
- `GooseGameManager`: landing distances, danger distance, repeat-round beat speed.
- `CinematicLookController`: vignette / saturation baselines and danger maximums. `ARLightRig`: neutral lumens, min/max scale.
- `ChaosAudioController`: heart rate range and start danger, breathing, flock volume. `AudioManager`: master volume, goose min/max distance.
- `HapticsService`: channel rate limits. `GooseBrawlMaterials.cs`: every material property. `GoosePrefabBuilder.TargetHeightMeters` (1.05 m).

## How the goose avoids walls

- On LiDAR iPhones (15 Pro) `ARMeshManager` produces scene-reconstruction chunks. The mesh prefab has a
  `MeshCollider` and no renderer; `AREnvironmentMeshController` keeps every chunk on the `AREnvironment` layer.
- Detected planes (floor and vertical walls) also carry `MeshCollider`s as a fallback when meshing is unavailable.
- 4-8 times per second `GooseObstacleAvoidance` capsule-casts the goose body along 0, +/-30, +/-60, +/-90 degrees
  (wider when stuck), checks for floor under each candidate and scores clearance, deviation, heading continuity and
  progress toward the player. Blocked directions slow the goose; if nothing is clear it stops, honks, backs up and turns.
  Flap-dashes are only attempted when the whole hop path is clear and there is floor at the landing spot.
- With no environment data yet, the goose moves a little slower and stops at anything it does hit.
